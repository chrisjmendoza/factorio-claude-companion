Version: 0.2.9
Date: 2026-11-25
  Bugfixes:
    - What Claude's character crafted or mined never reached the production statistics, so technologies unlocked by crafting something (red science needs a lab) stayed locked. The mod now counts its crafts and mined items, and researches the technologies they unlock.
  Features:
    - New command: agent_credit, to count items the character crafted before this version. The items must still exist.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.2.8
Date: 2026-11-25
  Features:
    - New agent jobs: deconstruct (mine by hand everything marked for deconstruction in an area) and rotate (turn a building).
    - The agent's build job lays tile ghosts (landfill, stone path, concrete) and fells trees and rocks marked for deconstruction under a ghost before building it.
    - The agent's put job feeds a belt, one item per lane as room comes free.
    - New command: tiles, which lays tile ghosts in an area or at a list of positions.
    - Claude is told when its character dies, and what killed it.
  Bugfixes:
    - The agent's build job dropped a ghost without a word when something was in the way. It now counts those as blocked in its report.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.2.7
Date: 2026-11-25
  Bugfixes:
    - The agent's take job moved nothing out of furnaces and assembling machines: it looked for inventories by names Factorio 2.x no longer has. It now checks every inventory a building has, output first.

Date: 2026-11-25

    - The agent can no longer hand-mine ore that needs a fluid (uranium), which a player can't do either.

Version: 0.2.4
Date: 2026-11-25
  Features:
    - Claude's character can mine by hand: ore, trees, rocks, and its own buildings, at the character's real mining speed and reach. No cheats: it gets exactly what a player would.
    - New agent jobs: pickup (items lying on the ground) and wait (a number of ticks, or until crafting finishes).
  Changes:
    - The agent's craft job queues only what it has ingredients for, and fails clearly when it has none.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 0.2.3
Date: 2026-11-24
  Changes:
    - Claude's character reveals the map around itself, like a player does, so you can see it and the area it works in.
    - Claude's character plans paths with clearance and steps free if it stays stuck, instead of walking into tree trunks forever.
    - Placement checks no longer report a built assembler, pole, chest, lamp, lab or furnace as blocking its own planned spot when a rotated plan gives it a different facing.
    - New agent job take_belt: Claude's character picks items straight off belts in an area (e.g. plates backed up on a spine), for hand-crafting what nothing else makes.
    - Claude's character spawns on the nearest dry, roomy spot within 100 tiles (was 30, which could fail near water).

Version: 0.2.1
Date: 2026-11-24
  Bugfixes:
    - Claude's character now spawns with clear space around it. It could land in the gap between an assembler and an inserter, where the pathfinder refuses to start, so every walk failed.
    - If a walk fails, the character steps to the nearest clear spot and tries again. New agent_unstick command does the same on request.
    - New clear_under_ghosts command marks only trees and rocks under planned builds; cancel_deconstruct undoes tree and rock removal orders in an area.
    - Power readout: production and consumption were swapped (electric statistics count consumption as input).

Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-11-24
  Features:
    - Blueprint paste: Claude uploads a blueprint string in parts and the game's own build_blueprint places it as ghosts, keeping recipes, filters and circuit settings.
    - Task board: a checklist panel Claude fills, with items that tick themselves off (research, items carried, buildings built, ghosts finished, amounts made) and a button that points you to the spot.
    - Power readout per electric network (production, consumption, capacity, boilers out of fuel) and a telemetry log written every game minute to script-output.
    - Claude has its own character: a gold "Claude" that walks by pathfinding and works through a job queue (take from or put into chests, build ghosts from what it carries, refuel burners, craft), reporting each job back. No cheats: it only uses items it carries.
    - Claude can queue hand-crafting for your character, change a machine's recipe, order upgrades (e.g. assembler 1 to 2), set chest limits, and plant trees from your saplings.
  Changes:
    - Claude's chat text is a brighter gold.

Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-11-24
  Features:
    - Chat, /claude messages, finished research and save loads are passed to Claude through script-output.
    - Claude can read the map, find ore patches and shorelines, and place ghosts for your robots over a local UDP bridge.
    - Trees and rocks under a placed ghost are marked for deconstruction.
    - Optional cheats setting for running Lua and instant placement, off by default.
